# Asset credits

## Cards

`cards/*.png` are drawn by this project's own generator, `tool/make_cards.py` (vector suits, Poppins
indexes), so they are ours to ship. Names are the contract with the code: `<rank><suit>.png` with ranks
J 9 A T K Q 8 7 and suits C D H S, plus `back.png` (emerald) and `back_red.png` (maroon). Drop in new art
with the same names (140x190 aspect ratio) and nothing else changes.
