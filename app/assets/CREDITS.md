# Asset credits

## Cards

`cards/*.png` - "Boardgame Pack" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), licensed CC0 1.0 (public domain).
Files were renamed to the game's card codes: `<rank><suit>.png` with ranks J 9 A T K Q 8 7 and suits C D H S,
plus `back.png` / `back_red.png`.

These are placeholders: drop in replacement art with the same file names (140x190 aspect ratio).

## Fonts

`fonts/Poppins-*.ttf` - Poppins by Indian Type Foundry, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(see `fonts/OFL.txt`). Weights bundled: 400, 500, 600, 800.

## Icons

App icons (`android/.../ic_launcher.png`, `web/icons/*`, `ios/.../AppIcon.appiconset/*`) are generated
from the app's own palette by `tool/make_icons.py`.
